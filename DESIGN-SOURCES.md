The React components were ported to semantic server-rendered HTML, local CSS, and vanilla JavaScript to retain the existing authentication and enrollment backend. Source effect styles live in `public/assets/supplied-effects.css`; responsive integration lives in `public/assets/final-design.css`.

The previously generated interactive editing study was restored from the sibling `phoenix` project, with story, color, and motion chapters, playback, scrubbing, and raw/graded views. The prior photographic images are absent from rendered pages and the static export. Earlier implementation files are preserved in `output/design-backup/`.

Copy now connects editing practice to storytelling, live feedback, client work, and creative careers. No source-template income guarantees, fake popularity counts, discounts, or paid access claims were imported. Batch prices and schedules remain explicitly illustrative until confirmed in configuration.
